# Risk Assessment

## Overall Risk

**Risk Level: High**

## Potential Impact

If the employee followed the phishing link and entered their Microsoft
365 credentials, an attacker could potentially obtain access to the
employee's account.

A compromised account could potentially expose:

- Email communications
- Business documents
- Internal information
- Contacts
- Other connected Microsoft 365 resources

A compromised account could also potentially be used to send additional
phishing messages to other employees.

## Likelihood

The likelihood of successful social engineering is considered elevated
because the message combines:

- Brand impersonation
- Urgency
- Fear of account suspension
- A request for immediate action
- A link to an external verification page

## Risk Factors

1. Credential theft
2. Account compromise
3. Unauthorized access to business information
4. Internal phishing propagation
5. Potential business disruption

## Risk Reduction

Recommended controls include:

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Security awareness training
- Email filtering
- Phishing detection
- Domain and URL blocking
- User reporting mechanisms
- Monitoring for unusual account activity
